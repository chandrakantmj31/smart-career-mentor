## Brand & Style

This design system blends an editorial cadence with high-craft SaaS utility, engineered specifically for high-stakes career intelligence, resume audits, and trajectory tracking. Drawing structural ethos from platforms like Read.cv and Linear, the aesthetic embraces quiet authority, intellectual restraint, and intentional whitespace.

### Visual Principles
- **Editorial Typography & Hierarchy:** High-density analysis presented through immaculate typographic proportions and deliberate contrast, avoiding generic template tropes.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Layouts leverage razor-thin hairline borders, subtle tonal contrast, and strict alignment grids over heavy drop-shadows or saturated fills.
- **Technological Precision:** Micro-surfaces, contextual data visualization, and metric chips maintain a crisp, calculated presentation to reassure users of AI-driven analytical rigor.

## Colors

The palette balances cool, slate-derived minerals with crisp editorial paper surfaces. Color is applied with surgical restraint to signal status, metric progression, and primary actions without visual fatigue.

### Palette Implementation
- **Primary Accent (`#315C78`):** Deep Slate Teal. Reserved strictly for key conversion targets, high-level active states, focused form controls, and selected timeline intervals.
- **Secondary Accent (`#6C8F83`):** Calming Sage Green. Used for secondary analytical badges, peer comparison indicators, and affirmative AI insights.
- **Canvas & Structural Layers:** The primary viewport background (`#F7F8FA`) sets an off-white, tactile canvas, while cards, sidebars, and elevated analytical sheets sit on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) delineated by subtle divider lines (`#E7EBEF`).
- **Typography Inks:** Main body text (`#17212B`) delivers high-contrast legibility, supported by slate-tinted secondary text (`#77818D`) for meta-information, timestamps, and contextual hints.
- **Functional Semantics:** Success (`#36866C`) powers resume optimization scoring benchmarks; Warning (`#D59A45`) isolates gaps, keyword deficiencies, and formatting anomalies.

## Typography

Typographic execution separates narrative editorial hierarchy from technical precision.

- **Headlines (Manrope):** Geometric clarity with warm humanist proportions. Tightly kerned tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) lends a polished editorial profile to titles, audit headers, and persona summaries.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral workhorse type. Designed for deep-scan readability across analytical feedback, bullet-point annotations, and system dialogues.
- **Metrics & Data (IBM Plex Sans):** Technical, structured, and tabular-optimized. All quantitative indices (match scores, salary distributions, ATS parse confidence ratings) utilize IBM Plex Sans to project computational precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model pairs a fluid, modular analytical canvas with a fixed technical rail structure.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Dashboard & Workspaces:** Desktop uses a fixed 260px navigation and contextual panel rail alongside a 12-column fluid grid canvas with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer page margins. Maximum readable report width is capped at `1280px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** Grid drops to 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. The primary sidebar collapses to a slim icon utility bar (64px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Reflows to a single-column linear feed with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` margins. Complex comparative charts and multi-pane resume diff inspectors swap to vertical step flows or swipeable tab segments.
- **Rhythm:** Internal component paddings strictly conform to 8pt geometric increments (`space-sm` for compact tags, `space-md` for inputs and action tiles, `space-lg` for card surfaces, and `space-xl` between logical audit sections).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual plane differentiation relies on hairline boundaries, tactile layering, and ambient light distribution rather than stark, heavy elevations.

- **Hairline Division (Base Flat):** Content cards, inspectable resume tiles, and data groups live on `#FFFFFF` surfaces separated by a crisp `1px solid #E7EBEF` border with zero box shadow.
- **Soft Ambient Float:** Hoverable action cards, context menus, and popovers employ a diffuse, micro-tuned shadow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(23, 33, 43, 0.05), 0px 1px 3px rgba(23, 33, 43, 0.02)`.
- **Modals & Overlays:** Interactive resume preview overlays and AI generation sidebars use a tinted scrim (`rgba(23, 33, 43, 0.3)` with a `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`) paired with a high-plane card elevation: `0px 16px 40px -8px rgba(23, 33, 43, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a disciplined, subtle corner rounding (`0.25rem` base), reinforcing an architectural, editorial print aesthetic.

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Data Tags:** `0.25rem` (4px). Clean, crisp corners keep interactive interfaces sharp and utilitarian.
- **Cards, Panels, and Containers (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px). Softens larger surface blocks while maintaining alignment integrity alongside adjacent grid panels.
- **Modals and Deep-View Sheets (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (12px). Applied strictly to root containers of detached, floating elements.
- **Pill Exception:** Metric status badges, AI generation indicator pills, and tag filters may leverage full-radius pill styling (`rounded-full`) to delineate them from structured input surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#315C78` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` radius. Height 38px (medium) with `0.875rem` weight 500 Inter typography. Subtle hover transition shifts to `#264960`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with `1px solid #E7EBEF`, text in `#17212B`. On hover, background shifts to `#F7F8FA` with border tinting to `#315C78`.
- **Destructive / Flag:** Soft muted red tint background with deep crimson label for dangerous resume discard operations.

### Cards & Analytical Modules
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #E7EBEF` perimeter. Padding set to `1.5rem` (`space-lg`).
- **Interactive Variants:** Resume revision cards display a top border hairline or left accent bar (`#315C78`) when highlighted, paired with the soft ambient hover shadow.

### Input Fields & Selects
- **Default State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E7EBEF`, font size `0.9375rem`, padding `0.625rem 0.875rem`. Placeholder text in `#77818D`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to `#315C78` with a discrete outline ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(49, 92, 120, 0.12)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Skill / Metric Tags:** Compact padding (`0.25rem 0.5rem`), font size `0.75rem` (`IBM Plex Sans` for metrics, `Inter` for skills).
- **Match Benchmark (High):** Subtle tint of `#36866C` at 10% opacity, text in `#36866C`, `rounded-full`.
- **ATS Risk / Warning:** Subtle tint of `#D59A45` at 12% opacity, text in `#B88030`, `rounded-full`.

### Resume Audit Diff & Annotation Lists
- **List Items:** Horizontal rows partitioned by hairline borders (`#E7EBEF`). Includes a monospaced metric column on the left (e.g., impact score +14%), human-readable feedback in the center, and a quick-action trigger button right-aligned.
- **AI Feedback Callout:** Inset card with `#F7F8FA` fill, left accent border of `3px solid #6C8F83`, and inline markdown capability.
